WHO WE ARE

Building trust through every project.

UK Renovators CC began as a renovations business in 2004 and has grown into a broader building and construction company. We serve clients across residential, commercial, industrial and maintenance projects.

Our approach centres on customer service, accountability, teamwork, safe working practices and professional delivery.
